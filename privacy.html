2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Prompt content — your messages to the API are not stored or logged.
  • Model outputs — completions are not retained after delivery.
  • Browsing behaviour — we do not use third-party tracking pixels or analytics.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Service.
  • To calculate and charge for usage.
  • To send transactional emails (verification, receipts, low-balance alerts).
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Inference Data

When you call /v1/chat/completions, your prompt is forwarded to our GPU inference backend and the completion is returned to you. Neither the prompt nor the response is logged or stored. The only data recorded is token counts and cost for billing purposes.

6. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12).
  • All data in transit is encrypted via TLS.
  • API keys are stored hashed and shown once at creation.
  • Accounts lock after 10 failed login attempts.
  • Disposable email addresses are blocked at registration.
  • Stripe Radar fraud detection is active on all payments.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Access your data via the dashboard or by emailing us.
  • Correct inaccurate information in account settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by emailing us.
  • Export your usage logs from the dashboard.

8. Cookies

We use only essential session cookies required for authentication. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated by email at least 14 days before they take effect.
